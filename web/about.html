About

Some things are easier
to say to a stranger.

deadletter is a place for the words you've been carrying — the apology you never gave, the truth you're afraid to tell, the thought that keeps you up at 3am.

You text us via iMessage. An AI — not a human — reads your message and writes back with empathy and care. Then your words are anonymized and published here, where strangers can find comfort in knowing they're not alone.

No sign-up. No app. No data stored. Just a text message and a chance to let go.

Privacy

Your phone number is hashed and never stored in plain text. All personal information is removed using AI-powered anonymization before anything is published. We can't identify you, and we don't want to.
