How it works
01

Text your truth

Send an iMessage to our number. Say what you've been holding in — a confession, a worry, a question you can't ask anyone else.

02

AI writes back

Within moments, you receive a thoughtful reply. Not generic advice — a real conversation about what you shared. Keep talking as long as you need.

Your message is stripped of all personal info and published anonymously on this site. Your name, number, location — gone. Only your words remain.

People read letters from strangers and realize they're not alone. Your unsent words become someone else's solace.
